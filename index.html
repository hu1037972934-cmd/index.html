Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · GDPR-compliant

Proposly ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains, in plain language, what we collect, why, and the rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. No account, no personal data

You do not need to register or log in. We do not collect your name, email, or any identifying information when you use the free generator. Your daily usage quota is stored only in your browser's local storage on your device.

2. What we process

  • Job inputs: the job title and description you paste are sent to our server only to generate your proposal and are not stored after the response is returned.
  • Local storage: a small counter tracking your daily free usage is kept on your device. It contains no personal data.
  • Cookies: we do not set tracking or advertising cookies. No third-party analytics run on this site.

3. Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6)

We process the job text you submit on the basis of contract performance — it is necessary to deliver the service you requested. We do not process special-category data.

4. Data retention

Generated proposals are returned to you and not retained on our servers. You may copy them; we do not keep copies. The local quota counter persists until you clear your browser data.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, plus the right to data portability and to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. Because we hold no personal data about anonymous users, most of these rights are satisfied automatically — but you can contact us anytime at privacy@proposly.app and we will respond within 30 days.

6. International transfers

Generation requests are processed by our infrastructure and our AI provider, which may be located outside the EU/EEA. Transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by the European Commission, with supplementary measures to ensure an adequate level of protection.

7. Children

Proposly is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly process their data.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

Questions about your data? Email privacy@proposly.app. We are the controller responsible for your personal data.
